================================================================================
CONCLUSION
================================================================================

The missing 22% of weather data is entirely explained by the lack of weather
station coverage for 99 ZIP codes (affecting 218 meters). This is a data
source limitation, not a data processing error. All meters have proper ZIP
code assignments.

The issue can be mitigated through intelligent imputation strategies, with
nearest neighbor and regional averaging being the most practical approaches
given the Slovak geographic context and available regional classifications.
